Calculate the form by hand

For a 20 m × 20 m building, 3 m high, calculate the roof area, external wall area, enclosed volume and exposed surface-to-volume ratio. Exposed area here excludes the ground floor.

Reveal worked calculation

Roof = 20 × 20 = 400 m²
Walls = 2 × (20 + 20) × 3 = 240 m²
Exposed area = 400 + 240 = 640 m²
Volume = 400 × 3 = 1,200 m³
S/V = 640 / 1,200 = 0.533 m⁻¹

Now compare 40 m × 10 m: roof = 400 m²; walls = 300 m²; exposed area = 700 m²; volume = 1,200 m³; S/V = 0.583 m⁻¹.

The 5 m daylight reach and 12 m ventilation depth are illustrative assumptions. Actual performance depends on glazing, orientation, wind, obstructions and detailed design. Greater envelope area only implies greater transmission heat flow when the relevant U-values and temperature differences are comparable.
