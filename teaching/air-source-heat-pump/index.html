Q_air = ṁ × L_usable = 0.0285 kg/s × 200 kJ/kg = 5.7 kW
  • Enters at 2.4 bar and -15°C as a cold liquid-vapour mix.
  • Because -5°C outside air is 10°C warmer than the refrigerant, heat flows into the coil and boils the liquid.
  • Leaves as a low-pressure gas at 2.4 bar, -10°C (263 K), expanding to 5.35 litres per cycle.
Stage Component Inlet State Outlet State Energy & Key Physics
Step 1 Outdoor Evaporator 2.4 bar, -15°C (Mix) 2.4 bar, -10°C (Gas, 5.35 L) Absorbs 5.7 kW free heat from -5°C outdoor air (Latent Heat)
Step 2 Compressor 2.4 bar, -10°C (263 K, 5.35 L) 17.0 bar, +75°C (348 K, 1.00 L) Uses 2.3 kW electricity; Combined Gas Law spikes temp to +75°C
Step 3 Indoor Condenser 17.0 bar, +75°C (Hot Gas) 17.0 bar, +45°C (Liquid) Delivers 8.0 kW (5.7 + 2.3 kW) to 40°C→45°C water (COP = 3.5)
Step 4 Expansion Valve 17.0 bar, +45°C (Liquid) 2.4 bar, -15°C (Mix) Drops pressure 7×; 48% flash-boils to self-cool back to -15°C
