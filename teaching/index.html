Design ideaWhat it changes
Insulation / U-valueResistance to heat passing through the building envelope.
Thermal massEnergy stored per degree of temperature change.
Solar shadingHow much solar radiation reaches the glazing.
VentilationAir exchange and the resulting heat transfer between indoors and outdoors.

These are simplified teaching models, with assumptions and limits stated in each activity. Use them to understand cause and effect, rather than to predict the performance or regulatory compliance of a real building.
